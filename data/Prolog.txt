PROLOG
================================================================

SECTION 1: WHAT IS PROLOG
Prolog is a logic programming language developed in the 1970s by Alain Colmerauer and his team. It is based on formal logic and uses a declarative programming paradigm. Prolog is primarily used in artificial intelligence, expert systems, and natural language processing. It is an interpreted language, which means that the code is executed line by line, and it is dynamically typed.

SECTION 2: WHY LEARN PROLOG
1. Prolog is used in various industries such as artificial intelligence, robotics, and natural language processing.
2. Knowledge engineers and AI researchers use Prolog to build expert systems and knowledge graphs.
3. Prolog is used in academia for research in areas like computer science, linguistics, and cognitive science.
4. Companies like IBM, Oracle, and Cisco have used Prolog in their products and services.
5. Prolog is used in the development of intelligent systems, such as chatbots and virtual assistants.
